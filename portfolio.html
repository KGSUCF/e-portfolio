About

Karen Strouse has spent more than three decades helping telecommunications providers navigate competitive markets. Her practice spans marketing strategy, business planning, mergers and acquisitions, executive education, and organizational redesign — with clients ranging from Fortune 25 carriers to emerging-market wireless startups across Latin America, the Middle East, and Asia.

Before founding her independent practice she served as Consulting Manager at Deloitte & Touche and held senior roles at Bell Atlantic and Bell of Pennsylvania. She is the author of three industry books published by Artech House, a prolific contributor to telecom trade journals, and a speaker at international industry conferences.
